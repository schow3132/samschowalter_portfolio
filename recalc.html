Why Recalc

A Community I Want to Invest My Time In.

Recalc is a platform I hope to put a lot of time toward, and I want to make the most of it.

The community

A selective process

I like that Recalc is selective. It tells me the people in it are serious about the work, and it makes a spot something to make the most of. If I'm part of it, I plan to put real time and energy into it.

Right now

Helping students through recruiting

I hope to help other students in Recalc during the recruiting process. I care deeply about collaboration: I co-founded a program at UMN Duluth that prepares first- and second-year students for finance recruiting.

Over a career

Giving back

I want to achieve at a high level in my career, because many of the people I've connected with have been crucial to where I am today. I want to be that resource for the students who come after me, and hope to give back to Recalc in the future.

What I'd bring

How I'd Show Up.

Curiosity across strategies

I'd like to understand several investing philosophies, because each one shows a different side of how dynamic business ownership is. I'd rather understand investing as a whole, not one strategy repeated over and over.

I build alongside people

I co-founded a program to help younger students break into finance because I'd have wanted one. I'd like to learn from my peers and take in new perspectives, and see where I have room to grow too.

Follow-through

I've been the person others count on, on job sites and on the ice. If I say I'll do something, it gets done.
