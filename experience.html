Experience

Learning by Doing.

I've tried to build both sides of the career I want, with operating experience at CentiMark and investing experience at Minnesota.

Operating

Managing Operations.

At CentiMark I worked on large commercial and industrial roofing projects, the kind of job where a crane, a crew, the right material, and a client's schedule all have to line up on the same day. I started in the warehouse and ended up coordinating the jobs themselves.

Roofing taught me that a business is a long series of small decisions, and that they all show up in the numbers eventually. That's the lens I bring to investing.

Investing

The Foundation I'm Building.

Since arriving at Minnesota, I've looked for every chance to practice investing, not just study it. Here's how that has looked so far.

Inside a private equity firm

This past summer I worked alongside a private equity team in Minneapolis on live deals. Much of it was learning an industry quickly, then digging into a company's numbers, from its customers to its financial history, to understand what was actually going on. It was my first close look at how a firm decides what to buy.

Pitching to a real fund

I covered the industrials sector for a student-managed fund at UMN Duluth, pitching ideas, defending them, and helping decide what to keep. After years around operations, it was a natural place to look at companies from the investor's side.

Building a valuation from scratch

I built a full financial model and valuation of QXO from the ground up, then presented my thesis and recommendation to the Analyst Development Program.

Investing my own money

I invest my own money too, tracking how each position affects the portfolio's overall risk and rebalancing as I learn what works. Aerospace and defense names have been some of the standouts.

Helping younger students get started

I co-founded a program to help first- and second-year students prepare for finance recruiting. We brought banks and private equity firms from Minneapolis to meet students, and I worked with students on their resumes and mock interviews.

Talking markets with a club

As an officer of the investment club, I led discussions on sectors and companies and helped members weigh ideas against each other. I also competed in a simulated trading competition and finished near the top.
